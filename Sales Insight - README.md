# 📈 Sales Insights Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Overview
A business sales performance dashboard built in **Power BI** to analyze revenue and quantity across regions, customers, and products.  
The dashboard helps uncover top-performing areas and customers, track trends over time, and provide actionable insights for sales growth.  

---

## 🔑 Key Features
- 💰 **KPIs:** Total Revenue & Sales Quantity  
- 🌍 **Revenue & Quantity by Region**  
- 📅 **Sales Trend over Time** (Year/Month slicers)  
- 👥 **Top 5 Customers** and **Top 5 Products**  
- ⚡ Interactive filters for drill-down analysis

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- **Power BI Desktop** – for dashboard creation  
- **SQL** – for data preparation  
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – for measures and calculations  

---

## 🚀 Outcome
This dashboard enabled:  
- Identification of **top revenue-generating regions & customers**  
- Tracking of **sales trends over time** for seasonal performance  
- Insights into **high-performing products** to guide business strategy  
- Better decision-making through **visual and interactive sales reporting**  

---

## 📂 How to Use
1. Clone/download this repo.  
2. Open the `.pbix` file in **Power BI Desktop**.  
3. Connect your own sales dataset to refresh the visuals.
